# ETM Family Home
Open https://etm2026.github.io/etm-whiteboard/ on each device.

## Put this revision online

If you already use Family Home, extract this ZIP and upload its contents to the existing ETM repository, keeping the folder structure. Once GitHub Pages finishes, reopen your usual address on both devices. This revision needs no new Worker settings, database changes or backup import.

For the first upgrade from an older family setup:

1. Extract the ZIP. Upload its contents into the root of ETM2026/etm-whiteboard on main, keeping the existing folders. Commit the files. Keep your existing deployment configuration and secrets.
2. Wait for GitHub Pages and the Cloudflare Worker build to complete. Close and reopen ETM on the laptop where Dad’s music is already saved. Choose Dad and enter the existing PIN. Leave it open until “Family sign-in is ready” appears and the header shows “Saved”. The existing library stays in place and uploads in the background.
3. On the phone or a school computer, open the same address. Emily chooses Emily and enters her own PIN. Guest works without family sign-in. On a new personal phone Dad can select Dad, connect using his existing PIN and then unlock his tools. If an old phone shows the wrong family, use “Sign in to the existing family” at the profile chooser.

## During a visit
* On a school computer use Finish visit when done. A visit on a new computer needs internet to open and save family work. The child’s room is kept in memory and locks after ten idle minutes. Let saving finish before closing the tab. Guest activities are separate.
* In Music → My pieces, Dad can select Share piece and choose a family member or Everyone. Each recipient gets a separate arrangement and practice history.
* Both people should open the updated app before making a call. In Together, choose a person for a voice or video call. They tap Answer. The call panel shows the participant and provides microphone, camera (during video) and end controls. School networks can restrict direct calls.
* In Chat choose the recipient. A banner, unread count and optional chime alert the recipient during their ETM visit. Closed-app push notifications are not included.

## Explore & Friends

- On Home, select **Santa’s List** just below the welcome panel. It opens the same saved wishes and letter as the earlier list.
- In **Geography**, choose **Explore Ireland** or **Explore Australia**. Tap a map pin or place name to learn about it. Choose **Let me try three questions** to play on the map and record progress. Ireland and Australia are also topics in Family quiz.
- **Across the oceans**, **Our wonderful world** and **Little world journeys** now respond directly to map taps. Science’s **A drop’s adventure** shows the stages of the water cycle.
- In **Games & competitions → Play with a friend**, choose Three in a row or Connect four and copy the invitation. Send it privately to one friend. The friend opens it, chooses Guest or their own profile and taps **Join my friend**. The host taps **Let them in** after recognising their name. Keep both tabs open.
- Both players can chat, mute chat and leave. The Friend room button shows unread messages while you use another activity. Guests join a separate two-player room. Family calls, screen shares and saved work use their existing private sessions. A school network can block peer connections.
- This revision changes the web app. Existing profile PINs and saves carry forward. Map shapes are adapted from public-domain Natural Earth 1:110m and 1:50m data. Coastlines and ocean divisions are simplified for learning.
